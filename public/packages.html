Package Policies & Limketkai FAQ

Everything you need to know about reserving rituals at our Cagayan de Oro sanctuary.

What deposit is required to secure a signature package reservation? expand_more
All package appointments require a 20% advance confirmation deposit via GCash, Maya, or bank transfer. The remaining balance can be settled at our Limketkai reception counter upon check-in via cash, card, or e-wallet.
Can I reschedule my appointment or swap specific treatments? expand_more
Yes! Rescheduling is permitted up to 12 hours prior to your scheduled time without forfeiture of deposit. Package elements (such as swapping Swedish massage pressure for traditional Filipino Hilot) can be adjusted upon consultation with your aesthetician.
Where inside Limketkai Center is True Radiance located? expand_more
We are nestled peacefully along Rosario Crescent, Limketkai Center (near the garden concourse walkway). Free dedicated valet and ample mall parking are accessible just steps from our private reception foyer.
How early should we arrive before our package begins? expand_more
We recommend arriving 15 minutes early. This allows you to slip into complimentary linen robes, savor our chilled botanical tea infusion, and complete a customized skin and tension assessment.
