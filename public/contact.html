Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about reservations, VIP room bookings, payment, and arrival protocols.

Are walk-in appointments accommodated at the Limketkai branch?expand_more
Yes, walk-ins are welcome subject to specialist and suite availability. To guarantee your preferred time and specialist, we recommend reserving online or through our Viber concierge.
How do I secure an appointment reservation with GCash or Maya?expand_more
Reserve your slot online, then our concierge confirms it and shares deposit details. You can settle your deposit by GCash or Maya, and the balance at the sanctuary reception by cash, card or e-wallet.
Can I reserve the entire VIP Suite for bridal entourage preparations?expand_more
Absolutely. Our private suites are ideal for bridal parties, pre-wedding glam sessions, and private celebrations. Message our concierge on Viber or use the inquiry form above so we can plan your timings.
Is dedicated client parking available at Rosario Crescent?expand_more
Yes. You may park along Rosario Crescent or within the Limketkai Center parking areas, and our concierge can assist at the entrance. Let us know in advance if you need an escort after late-evening treatments.
