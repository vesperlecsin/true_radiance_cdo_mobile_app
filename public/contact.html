Guest Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick details to assist your upcoming visit to our Limketkai beauty sanctuary.

Is guest parking available near your Limketkai branch?
expand_more
Yes, extensive parking is directly accessible. You may park at the open surface parking right along Rosario Crescent, the Limketkai Luxe Hotel designated guest area, or the multi-level Limketkai Mall parking bays. We also offer escort assistance to your vehicle upon request after late-evening spa treatments.
What payment options do you accept? Do you support GCash & Maya?
expand_more
We happily accommodate all major local cashless transactions including GCash, Maya (PayMaya), BDO/BPI QR Ph transfers, as well as Visa and Mastercard credit or debit cards. Cash settlement in Philippine Pesos (₱) is always accepted at the sanctuary reception.
Can I reschedule or adjust my reserved appointment?
expand_more
We understand plans can change. You may reschedule without penalty up to 4 hours prior to individual appointments by sending a quick message to our Viber concierge (+63 917 892 4561) or calling (088) 856-7890. For bridal entourage and full-day suites, we appreciate 24 hours notice.
Do you accommodate private bridal entourages or corporate spa retreats?
expand_more
Absolutely. Our Limketkai salon features dedicated VIP suites designed for bridal parties, pre-wedding glam sessions, and private wellness celebrations complete with complimentary floral herbal infusions. Reach our direct bridal coordinator on Viber or through our consultation form above.
